Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Is this an official eligibility approval?

No. The AI Advisor provides preliminary suggestions only. Final eligibility must be confirmed by CTrustGlobal's advisory team and, where applicable, the relevant government authority.

Will the AI tell me the exact best country?

It will narrow your possible directions based on your answers. Some profiles may receive one clear direction, while others may require manual advisor review.

Can it compare citizenship and residency options?

Yes. The Advisor is designed to assess both Citizenship by Investment and Residency by Investment pathways, including Golden Visa-style programs.

Can my family be included?

Many programs allow family inclusion, but rules differ by country, dependent age, relationship, and financial dependency. Complex family cases should always be reviewed manually.

How long does the assessment take?

The questionnaire is designed to take approximately 3–5 minutes, depending on whether conditional questions appear based on your answers.

What happens after I complete it?

You receive preliminary AI suggestions and can request a Personalized Freedom Plan from CTrustGlobal's advisory team with no obligation.
